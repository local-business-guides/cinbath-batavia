What to discuss before work begins

The details vary from one bathroom to another. A conversation about your project may cover the tile areas, the overall look you’re after, and any concerns about aging fixtures, limited storage, or signs of moisture damage. If you’re also considering a new vanity, shower, tub, floor, or lighting, mention those plans together so the work can be considered as a whole.

A simple preparation checklist can help:
- Write down the parts of the bathroom you want changed.
- Gather a few examples of tile styles or colors you like.
- Point out visible damage or areas that concern you.
- Share which bathroom features matter most for comfort and everyday use.
- Ask what decisions you’ll need to make before the project moves ahead.

Try not to choose tile based on appearance alone. A common mistake is to focus on one finish before explaining how you want the room to function. It’s also worth mentioning moisture concerns instead of treating them as a separate issue from the renovation.
